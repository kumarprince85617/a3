Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Published by Syntax Harbor Institute LLC

This Cookie Policy clarifies the deliberate methodologies through which Syntax Harbor Institute LLC, maintaining principal academic operations at 1999 Avenue of the Stars, Suite 2800, Los Angeles, CA 90067, United States, deploys cookies and associated browser memory technologies across our educational domain. These small digital tokens are safely saved to your client device to sustain authentication sessions, remember academic track preferences, and accelerate page loading speeds across our research repository. By continuing to navigate our educational portal without modifying your browser settings, you consent to our operational use of these technologies.

We implement functional cookies that are strictly essential for platform security, encrypted admissions transmission, and interactive FAQ displays across all desktop and mobile viewports.
